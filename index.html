03 · Experimental Design

A fair fight: one environment, one budget, four mechanisms

WHY PURE NUMPY?

Wrapping a 500-state problem in deep networks mostly measures optimizer luck. Implementing all four agents from scratch with exact / one-hot state representations makes this a controlled ablation: any difference comes from replay + target sync, clipped policy updates or max-entropy twin critics — nothing else.

2,500
Training episodes

≤ 200 steps each, identical valid starts, seed=42.

500
Greedy test episodes

No exploration (ε=0), unseen starts (seed=999).

4
Metrics

Return, steps, total −10 penalties, episodes to reach +5.0.

12×3
Sensitivity runs

12 settings of α, γ, ε, each averaged over 3 seeds.

04 · Agent 01

SARSA — learns from the move it actually makes

How it behaves on Taxi-v4
  • On-policy and cautious. It bootstraps on the real next action A′, so its values include the cost of its own random exploration.
  • One step of credit per update. The +20 only creeps back one cell at a time — hundreds of episodes to reach the start.
  • Blind exploration hurts. Uniform ε-greedy picks an illegal Pickup/Dropoff about ε/3 of the time → 10,908 penalties.
  • But it gets there. Once ε decays to 0.01 it drives the exact optimal policy: +7.01, 100%.

04 · Agent 02

DQN — remembers the past and replays it

  • Replay buffer (12,000). Every transition is stored; random 32-sample mini-batches are replayed every 4 steps, breaking correlation.
  • Target network (sync every 150 steps). Bootstrap targets stay still long enough to learn from.
  • Result: 596 episodes to +5.0 — the fastest of all four, 37% faster than SARSA.
  • Still ε-greedy. It keeps making random illegal moves early: 7,192.

05 · Agent 03

PPO — improves the policy, but never too much at once

  • Actor + critic. A softmax actor outputs π(a|s); the critic scores each move with advantage A = Q − V.
  • The clip. If the new/old probability ratio leaves [0.8, 1.2] the update is skipped — 3 epochs per episode.
  • Safer than ε-greedy. The softmax quickly stops choosing −10 moves: 3,606 penalties (−67% vs SARSA).
  • Price of being on-policy. Old episodes are thrown away → 1,023 episodes; 99.6% greedy success.

05 · Agent 04

Discrete SAC — explores curiously, but never stupidly

  • Twin critics. Bootstraps on min(Q₁, Q₂) to avoid over-optimistic values.
  • Entropy bonus. Rewards keeping options open; temperature τ anneals 1.2 → 0.08.
  • The key effect. A move worth −10 gets weight e^(−10/τ) ≈ 0 — exploration stays among sensible moves. Only 1,486 penalties.

07 · When Learning Breaks

Three knobs, three classic failure modes

α · learning rate
sweet spot 0.20 – 0.50

0.05 crawls. 0.90 bounces around −100 — each noisy target overwrites what it learned.

γ · discount
sweet spot 0.95 – 0.99

0.50 shrinks +20 to 0.5¹³ ≈ 0.0001 by the start — the pickup stage never hears about the reward.

ε · exploration
best: decay 1.0 → 0.01

Fixed 0.30 never stops making −10 mistakes (stuck below −25). Decay explores early, exploits late.

Limitations
  • Small and tabular. 500 states fit in a table — no generalisation needed. Images or continuous positions would need neural networks.
  • Deterministic world. Real dispatch has random travel times, traffic and road closures.
Next steps
  • Bigger cities. 20×20+ grids with PyTorch Double-DQN and neural SAC.
  • Messier cities. Stochastic traffic, fuel limits and cooperating multi-taxi fleets.
